# Surface: Skylike app (index.html)

Scope: the whole single-file app: tablet family screen (landscape) and phone companion. Mode: Operate.
Audience: a Perth family, two parents (one ADHD, one checklist-minded), two pre-reader kids, babysitters. Job: know now/next at a glance, keep lists and notes, each parent opens their own view.
Constraints: Safari iPad/iPhone + Chrome Android; always-on; must not feel busy, techy, or bright at night.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Today is a sky. A band across the top is the real day's sky from 6am to 9pm, tinted by the actual time of day, with events sitting on its horizon and a sun (moon after dark) marking now. Refuses the category default of white cards over a colour-coded agenda grid.

OWN-WORLD: Perth coast. Cool sea-mist ground (not cream), deep Indian Ocean blue rail, limestone sand fields, sunset apricot for now, family colours from local flora: jacaranda, wattle, eucalypt, banksia, ocean. Rounded system face (SF Pro Rounded on Apple), big tabular numerals, soft-offset shadows, generous radius, few borders. Night shifts the whole palette to dusk navy at low luminance.

STORY: From across the room: time, weather, what's next and who it's for. Up close: tap your face to get your own view; lists and notes are one tap away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left ocean rail with family faces, view switcher and sync dot. Top 34%: sky band with large clock and date at left, weather at right, events as pills on the horizon, sun at now. Below: Up next (three events, large), Lists preview, Notes board, with reminder chips (bin night, countdowns, school holidays) leading the lower area.

FORM: Coastal light, candidate 3 of 7 on the ordered list (assigned by roll; user chose it over the visual-timetable pick and the canon). seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
