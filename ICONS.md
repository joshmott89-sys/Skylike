# Icon provenance

`icon-180.png`, `icon-192.png` and `icon-512.png` were drawn programmatically with Python Pillow (a sky gradient, a sun setting on a sea horizon) by Claude while building Skylike in November 2026. No stock or third-party imagery is used.

## In-app artwork (November 2026)

All drawn by hand as inline SVG by Claude for Skylike, no stock or third-party art:
- Assistant portraits (`PAV`): line marks on tinted tiles.
- Kids' picture tiles (`KART`): flat sticker-style drawings for routine steps, jobs, things to bring, timer presets and default rewards. Any emoji without a drawing still shows as the emoji.
- Avatar pictures (`FACE`): animals and favourite things, drawn to sit in a person's colour circle, with their letter beside it when they add one.
- Stars and the extra UI icons (repeat, lock, mobile, tablet, bug, idea, heart, help, car, snow, cart, family) follow the existing 24-grid line icon set.
