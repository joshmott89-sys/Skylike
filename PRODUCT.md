# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Single-file static HTML/CSS/JS (no build step), deployed to GitHub Pages. Supabase (Sydney region) for auth, database, realtime, storage, edge functions and cron. Google Apps Script bridge for Google Calendar. Chosen with the user during planning; Josh develops from an Android phone, so no local toolchain is assumed.

## Users

- **Parent A** (the friend): has ADHD and already uses FocusFive. Needs today only, time-blindness help ("leave in X minutes"), very few items, fast capture.
- **Parent B** (partner): a checklist person. Wants today's checklist first, then the week, meals and lists.
- **Two kids, both under 6**: can't read yet. Anything aimed at them leads with pictures, colour and icons, not words.
- **Babysitters and visitors**: need emergency contacts, bedtimes, wifi and house rules in one tap.
- **Josh** (builder and admin): maintains it remotely; tests on an iPad and an Android phone.

Both parents carry iPhones; the family screen is a wall- or bench-mounted iPad or Android tablet, landscape, always on and plugged in.

## Product Purpose

One shared screen that removes the parents' mental load: what's on today, what's for dinner, what to bring, what needs doing. Success is both parents using it every day without being reminded, and no support visits after the first fortnight.

## Positioning

A subscription-free, ADHD-aware alternative to Skylight Calendar: each parent gets a home view shaped to how their mind works (Focus vs Planner), built on the Google Calendar the family already uses.

## Operating Context

- Read at a glance from across a room, often while doing something else (shoes, lunches, leaving the house).
- Always on in a family hallway or kitchen, including after dark.
- Parents act on it from their iPhones too (lists, notes, capture); the tablet is the shared view.
- Google Calendar on the parents' iPhones stays their calendar app; the hub mirrors and edits it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Phase 1 scope: clock, weather, countdowns, calendar display, lists and grocery, notes board, per-parent home views (Family, Focus, Planner), babysitter mode, bin-day / WA school term / public holiday reminders, night mode, photo screensaver.
- Later phases: chores, routines, stars, meal planner link, two-way calendar editing, brain-dump capture shared with FocusFive, push notifications, shop arrival alerts, motion wake.
- Must work in Safari (iPad, iPhone) and Chrome (Android). No native apps, no app stores.
- Free tiers only. AI keys are the family's own free keys with guided setup.
- No PIN or access control between family members.
- Each household's data is isolated by row level security.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Skylike (working name).
- Must not feel busy like a dashboard, cold or techy, or bright at night.

## Evidence on Hand

No real family content yet. Demo/sample data must be labelled as sample. No testimonials, pricing or claims exist.

## Product Principles

1. Glance first: the most important thing for right now is readable from across the room.
2. Less on screen beats more: hide what isn't needed today.
3. Each mind gets its own front door: Focus and Planner are different screens, not settings on one.
4. Calm at all hours: quiet by day, dim by night.
5. Pictures for little ones: anything for the kids works without reading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

ADHD-friendly design for Parent A (low clutter, time made visible). Pre-readers in the household. Large type and strong contrast for distance reading.
